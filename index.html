Uppsala AI Safety

We are a group focused on enabling individuals to limit the threat posed by powerful AI systems.

Do you think all of the following are true?

  • AI is developing rapidly.
  • Not enough is being done to oversee that rapid development.
  • The threat from a strong AI system without proper guardrails is high.

Join us! We want to spark interest in AI oversight and control, and provide an environment that enables individuals to contribute to AI safety.
